Needs pycairo, numpy, scipy, soundfile, Pillow, ffmpeg; font Luckiest Guy (raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/apache/luckiestguy/LuckiestGuy-Regular.ttf into ~/.fonts, fc-cache -f); Inter preinstalled.
