DESCRIPTION
A short film about loss: 40 days after his wife is gone, an old man still makes two cups of tea every morning.

Every morning the kettle boils, and every morning he takes down two cups. One for him, one for her. Her cup goes cold on the table, so he starts pouring it into the plant she loved by the window. Day by day the house stays quiet, until a neighbour knocks with soup and sits with him for a while.

This is an animated short film about grief and missing someone, told without a narrator, one morning at a time. It is not about getting over losing someone. It is about the small day when something changes anyway.

Small Win Films makes short animated films about everyday people. This film was animated entirely with code, with original music written for it.
