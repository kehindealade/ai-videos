"It's Not Just Me" relatable Reel engine (Small Win Films, built 24 Nov 2026, ep 1 "Every group chat ever"). Needs pycairo, numpy, scipy, soundfile, Pillow, ffmpeg; font Rounded Mplus 1c ExtraBold (raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/roundedmplus1c/RoundedMplus1c-ExtraBold.ttf into ~/.fonts, fc-cache -f).
gc_common.py: helpers, 8 drawn avatars, sprout logo. chat_reel.py (never name a file gc.py, it shadows the stdlib gc module): python3 chat_reel.py render silent.mp4 | still T out.png | cover out.png. gc_audio.py: python3 gc_audio.py writes mix_raw.wav (lo-fi e-piano, 16 bars at 101.05 bpm = 38.0 s, pings and ticks); then loudnorm two-pass to -16 LUFS and mux with ffmpeg. Full render takes about 40 s.
