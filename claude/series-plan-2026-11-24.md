## Why these lanes
- Pay: finance, insurance and legal lead YouTube CPMs ($15 to $50 in 2026 estimates); US viewers earn about 3 to 5 times more per view than viewers in India or Brazil (vidIQ, Apr 2026). Q4 has the highest CPMs, so long videos published in Dec earn more than the same views in Jan or Feb.
- Shorts pay little anywhere (US Shorts RPM about $0.07 average, finance maybe $0.15 to $0.25), so Shorts are for reach and for pointing at the long video. The money is in long videos of 8 minutes or more.
- Money Map's current audience is not US-first (Small Win Films 34.9% Philippines from the Shorts feed). To pull US viewers: topics with US institutions and dollar figures (FICO, IRS, 401(k), homeowners and auto insurance), language English (United States), and posting when US viewers are online.
- Gate: all topics explain how money works, no advice as fact, no politics, no named real people, no Nigerian names. Never promise monetization.
