## YouTube policy notes (17 Nov 2026)

- July 2026 guidance demonetizes three things: template-looking or repetitive uploads; unsatisfying or off-putting formulas (emotional manipulation, imitations, shock); AI personas posing as human experts on health, law, finance or politics. Money Map explains how money works and never gives advice as an expert. Detection is at whole-channel level.
- Since 1 Oct 2026 the Shorts feed gives less reach to re-upload channels.
- Monetization needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days; mid-roll ads need 8 minutes or more. Shorts pay roughly $0.03 to $0.10 per 1,000 views.
- Upload answers: not made for kids, category Education, altered content No (animation, synthetic voice not modelled on a real person).
