DESCRIPTION
Why is RAM so expensive? Makers are forecast to build about 26% more DRAM this year, yet prices keep climbing. The short version: AI data centers want high bandwidth memory (HBM), which uses roughly three times the chip-making space per gigabyte, so less capacity is left for phones and laptops.

Counterpoint Research said one common laptop module (8 GB DDR4) rose about 180% in a single quarter in early 2026, and Samsung said it expects the shortage to last through 2027, maybe 2028. Forecasts differ and none is a guarantee.
