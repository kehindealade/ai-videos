Christmas Animated Short Film | She Never Knocked

DESCRIPTION
Christmas animated short film about two siblings who stopped talking. After her brother missed her wedding, Maya drives to his house every Christmas Eve and never knocks. On the fourth, she does.

This short film about forgiveness and siblings has no narrator. The story is told with a few lines of text, a snowy street and one small counter in the corner: how many times she knocked.

It is a quiet Christmas cartoon short film for anyone who has waited for someone else to go first. If a family member, a brother or a sister, came to mind while you watched, you are the reason it was made.
