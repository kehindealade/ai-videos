TITLE (51 characters)
Social Security Tax Explained: Where Your 6.2% Goes

DESCRIPTION
Social Security tax explained: the 6.2% on your paycheck isn't saved in an account with your name. It pays this month's checks and earns you credits.

On a $60,000 salary you pay $3,720 a year and your employer matches it, $7,440 in total. About 85 cents of each dollar funds retirement and survivor benefits and the rest funds disability. In return you earn work credits: one for every $1,970 earned in 2027, up to four a year, and 40 credits (about ten years) to qualify.
