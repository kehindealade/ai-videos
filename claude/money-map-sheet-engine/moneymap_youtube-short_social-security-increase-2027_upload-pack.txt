Social Security Increase 2027: How Much Actually Lands?

DESCRIPTION
The Social Security increase for 2027 is 3.6%, about $75 a month for the average retiree, but Medicare Part B takes $6.50 of it back.

Average retired worker: $2,095 becomes $2,170 in January 2027 (SSA estimate). The standard Medicare Part B premium rises to $209.40 a month (CMS), and for most people on Medicare it comes straight out of the Social Security check, so the deposit is about $1,960. Your own numbers depend on your record and your premium.
