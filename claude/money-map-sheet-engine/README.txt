MONEY MAP SHEET ENGINE (long "How Social Security Works" plus 2 Shorts, built 24 Nov 2026)
Long look "Spreadsheet": light grid, column letters and row numbers, navy app bar, formula bar typing a formula per line, sheet tabs per chapter (Start, 1 to 6, Summary) with a teal "SHEET n" toast, gold selected-cell outline with fill handle. Fonts Archivo Black, DM Sans, Anonymous Pro.
Short 1 look "Adding-machine tape" (s1.py): desk, beige machine, tape printing lines in Liberation Mono (red for minus), pills for key numbers.
Short 2 look "Newspaper front page" (s2.py): newsprint, invented masthead "The Paycheck Post", Gelasio headlines, red kicker, halftone photo box with infographic per beat.
Files: ss_common.py (palette, helpers, check_paper, cnt), ss_content.py (53 lines with mood, per-line speed and gap), ss_engine.py (scenes, FX formulas, tabs, render, stills), ss_mix.py (keys, pops, chimes, 84 bpm pluck bed, room tone, srt, chapters.json), tts.py (Kokoro cache, en-gb for b voices), vcommon.py (vertical timeline, captions at y 1130-1150, render, audio), s1*.py, s2*.py, thumb.py.
Run long: python3 tts.py ss_content vo_long; python3 ss_mix.py ss_content vo_long long.wav long.srt; render 2 parts in parallel: python3 ss_engine.py render ss_content vo_long p0.mp4 0 2 (and 1 2), about 4 min; concat, mux with loudnorm I=-14. Stills: python3 ss_engine.py stills ss_content vo_long dir [mid|t1,t2].
Shorts: python3 tts.py s1_content vo_s1; python3 s1.py stills|cover|render (same for s2). Then mux s1_video.mp4 with s1.wav.
Voices: bf_alice 0.90 (long), af_alloy 0.99 (Short 1), af_nova 0.98 (Short 2). af_nicole was tried for Short 1 and dropped: whisper style, 57 s of speech for a 30 s script; not used in any video.
Gotchas: arrow() signature is (x1,y1,x2,y2,col,w,a,prog,head): passing prog in the a slot drew a full-height line. Archivo Black has no division sign, use DejaVu Sans. Fit long headlines with fit(). The yt-stats API sign-in could not be used here (credential embedding was blocked); the free autocomplete check works without sign-in.
