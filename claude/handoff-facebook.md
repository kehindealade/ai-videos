## Facebook rules to keep in mind
- AI label: Meta may require it for realistic AI voiceover (its own example is a reel narrated by a realistic AI voice). Label every Money Map and Small Win video. Cartoon visuals alone don't need it. Also use YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting.
- Original content only; no watermarked or recycled uploads; Meta's enforcement is automated and can misfire, so appeal if flagged.
- No engagement bait ("like if", "tag 3 friends", "share for part 2"), no get-rich claims, no bought followers, no spamming groups, don't delete and re-upload weak videos.
- Music only from Facebook's Sound Collection or royalty-free.
- Betting video: keep it educational (no app names, promo codes, referral links); gambling topics may get limited ads.
- External links (YouTube) reduce reach; keep the link in the Page's About section.
- Privacy: posts show only the Page name. Skip "Invite friends", hide the Page from the personal profile, don't interact from the personal account. Page Transparency shows creation date and admin country (Nigeria), not names.
