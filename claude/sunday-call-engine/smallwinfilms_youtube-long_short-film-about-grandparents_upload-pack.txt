TITLE (58 characters)
Every Sunday at 6, She Called | Short Film About Grandparents

DESCRIPTION
A short film about grandparents: every Sunday at 6, a grandmother calls her grandson. He lets it ring, and the counter in the corner keeps climbing.

He isn't cruel. He's busy: deadlines on Monday, a late train on Wednesday, dinner on the couch on Friday. Each time he sees the missed call, he means to call back later. In her kitchen, the second cup of tea goes out on the table and comes back to the shelf.

This animated short film about family love has no narrator. The story is told with a split screen, a wall clock that always reads 6:00, a red pen circle around every Sunday, and one line at the very end.

If someone came to mind while you watched, a grandma, a grandpa, a parent you keep meaning to ring back, this one is for you.
