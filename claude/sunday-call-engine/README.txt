SUNDAY-CALL ENGINE (Small Win Films film A "Every Sunday at 6, She Called", built 24 Nov 2026, build plan 24 Nov conversation 7)
Setup: pip install --break-system-packages pycairo soundfile scipy numpy librosa; fonts Rounded Mplus 1c ExtraBold, Bold, Medium from raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/roundedmplus1c/ into ~/.fonts, then fc-cache -f. For the voice: torch CPU, chatterbox-tts, kokoro-onnx (model files in /root/kokoro) with the two float32 patches (see claude/money-map-ram-engine/README.txt; in s3tokenizer/utils.py add audio = audio.float() right before the hann window line).
sd.py: palette (rose kitchen, teal night apartment), faces (gran with bun and gold glasses, grandson with fade), figures gran() and son(), props (tea cup, teapot, wall clock, mint wall phone, smartphone with down/incoming/missed/talk states), the red-marker counter counter(), and sets: kitchen, kitchen_bg, apt (desk, call, couch, sleep, table), office, train, close-ups (table_close with py offset, son_close, gran_close, keypad_close, hangup_close, calendar_close), card(), split() (16:9 split screen with the 6:00 clock on the divider).
film.py: SCENES timeline (43 shots, picture change every 2 to 3 s in the first 30 s, slow push on most shots via push(), clamped so no black edges), counter ticks (TICKS), captions, end card (paper calendar with sprout, "Who's still waiting for your call?"). python3 film.py render 0 90 p1.mp4 and 90 180 p2.mp4 in parallel, join with the concat demuxer; python3 film.py stills dir t...
sd_tts.py: Kokoro blend reference clips (son: bm_george+am_fenrir 0.40, gran: bf_alice+bf_emma 0.36) then Chatterbox Turbo for the one line and the laugh ("[laugh] [chuckle]"), into vo/. About 1 minute on 2 cores.
sd_audio.py: score (Rhodes, flute, upright bass, pad, brushes; 66 bpm 4/4; C minor Cm Ab Eb Bb / Cm Ab Fm G, then Eb major after the pick-up) plus effects (phone buzz, ringback, DTMF keypad, hang-up clunk, clock ticks, cup clinks, office, train, pour, marker squeak) plus the voice, ducked; writes sunday_audio.wav. Mux with loudnorm I=-16.5 TP=-1.5 (result -15.4 LUFS).
short.py: the one Short, 1080x1920 full bleed, vertical split (her kitchen on top, his side below) with a MISSED SUNDAYS band across the middle, hook plaque in the 384 to 900 band, captions in a dark pill at y 1190 to 1300, ends at week 5 6:00 PM before the pick-up, end card "Week 5. Does he pick up?". python3 short.py render out.mp4. sd_short_audio.py builds its audio from the film's music stem (96 to 134.8 s) plus new synced effects.
thumbs.py: thumbnail 1280x720 (his worried face left, ringing GRANDMA phone right, "HE LET IT RING.") and Short cover (split, "EVERY SUNDAY AT 6. HE LET IT RING.", MISSED SUNDAYS ?). thumbcheck.py copy included.
Gotchas: seated figures are hidden by laptops or monitors placed in front of their heads, so put the screen to the side; a camera push with an off-centre target leaves black edges unless clamped; in the vertical panels a scene centred low leaves black, so move the object (py) instead of the camera. Could not listen to the score or the voice.
