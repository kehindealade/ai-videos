Why Overdraft Fees Hit Broke People Hardest

DESCRIPTION
Overdraft fees explained: buy a $4 coffee with $3 in your account and your bank can charge you about $35 for covering the missing dollar.

That one overdraft fee wipes out roughly nine years of interest on $1,000 in an average savings account. Larger US banks collected about $2.96 billion in overdraft and similar bank fees in the first half of 2026, and these fees fall mostly on people who are already short of money. A few banks have dropped them. Many have not.
