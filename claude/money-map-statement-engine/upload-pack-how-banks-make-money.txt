How Banks Make Money From Your Savings (Not What You Think)

DESCRIPTION
How banks make money from your savings: you earn about $3.80 a year on $1,000, while the bank charges around $210 to lend that same money out.

In this video we follow one $1,000 deposit for a full year and count every way a bank gets paid along the way. First the spread, the gap between the interest a bank pays savers and what it charges on loans and credit cards. Then bank fees, including the $35 overdraft fee. Then interchange fees, the slice your bank takes every time you tap your card, and the investment banking and trading side most people never see. Finally we look at what happens when it breaks, using the Silicon Valley Bank collapse, and why deposit insurance exists.

If you have ever wondered how banks work, what banks do with your money, or why savings account interest is so low, this is the plain English answer.

Figures are US averages for 2026, since the US is where most people search this. The ideas work the same way almost everywhere.
