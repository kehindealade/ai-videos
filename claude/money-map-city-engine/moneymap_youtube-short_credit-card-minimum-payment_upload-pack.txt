TITLE (53 characters)
Credit Card Minimum Payment: What It Really Costs You

DESCRIPTION
A credit card minimum payment on $3,000 at 22% interest takes about 12 years to clear and costs roughly $4,000 in interest.

Rough example: $3,000 balance, 22% interest, minimum payment of interest plus 1% of the balance (at least $35), no new purchases. Month one is about $86, but about $56 is interest and only about $30 reduces what you owe. Total paid is about $7,000. Your card's rules and rate will differ; check your statement, which shows how long minimum payments take.
