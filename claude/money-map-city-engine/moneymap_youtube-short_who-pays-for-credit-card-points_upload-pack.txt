Who Pays for Credit Card Rewards? Not the Bank Alone

DESCRIPTION
Who pays for credit card rewards? On a $100 sale the store pays about $2.52 in fees, your bank keeps about $1.90 and sends you $1.50.

The store has to cover that fee somewhere, often in the price tag. A July 2026 NBER working paper (Egan, Matvos, Seru, Wang and Yao) estimates about $30 billion a year moves from cash and debit shoppers to card users, if stores pass fees into every price. It is one estimate with caveats, and the example numbers are typical, not a receipt.
