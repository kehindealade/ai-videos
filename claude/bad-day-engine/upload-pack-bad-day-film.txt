Nothing Could Fix His Bad Day | Animated Short Film

DESCRIPTION
A bad day short film: his boss shouted at him at 8 am, and after work he tried four things to feel better.

He bought his favourite food. He played football with his friends. He put on his favourite song. He took a long walk. None of it worked, and he went to bed still angry and still sad.

This is an animated short film about the kind of bad day that does not get fixed, told without a narrator, one hour at a time. Not every day can be saved. Some you just finish.

Small Win Films makes short animated films about everyday people. This film was animated entirely with code, with original music written for it.
