Why Is Printer Ink So Expensive? (Not Just the Ink)

DESCRIPTION
Why is printer ink so expensive? One HP 962 black cartridge worked out to $40.22 an ounce in a January 2025 comparison, about three times an 18-year-old Scotch.

The reason is the razor and blades model: one estimate said a $70 printer cost about $120 to build, so the money is in the refills. The cartridge is built to fit only that printer, and a study of ink costs says refills can add up to more than the printer's price within a year or two. You are not just buying ink, you are paying for the business model.
