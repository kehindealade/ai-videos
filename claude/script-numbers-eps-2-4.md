## Episode 2: First paycheck

Hook (number drop, new type for the series): frame 1 is a time clock punching a card that reads HOURS 80, with "80 hours. Her first paycheck." Question it raises: what does she do with it?
Story in one sentence: a young woman works 80 hours for her first paycheck, taxes take $190, and she spends $46.80 of it buying her mom dinner for the first time.
Gate: passes (adults, example pay stub labelled EXAMPLE, no advice, no state named, warm ending).

Look: "Diner shift". Teal and cherry diner palette with a kraft paper pay stub. Full-bleed scenes: diner floor, a bus at night, a restaurant booth. Device: a manila punch card that gets a purple stamp for every shift (counter: HOURS n on the card at y 318 to 414). Deduction lines peel off the pay stub as paper strips with a scissor snip. Fonts: Fredoka Bold for cards, Courier Prime for stub figures.
Characters: her, 22, light tan skin, dark wavy ponytail, teal server uniform with a cherry apron. Her mom, mid 50s, same skin, short grey-streaked bob, mustard cardigan.
Score: whistled lead over ukulele-style plucks, upright bass and soft claps, C major, 104 bpm, 18 bars = 41.5 s. Clock "chunk" and bell on frame 1, stamp thuds, register ding, scissor snips with a falling pitch per deduction, music drops to bass only at "Then the taxes came.", full whistle melody returns at the dinner.

Beats (time, picture, on-screen line):
1. 0.0 to 2.5: close-up, time clock slams the card, HOURS 80 big at centre then shrinks into the counter. "80 hours. Her first paycheck."
2. 2.5 to 4.5: card counter resets to 0; she ties her apron, first day, nervous smile. "Day 1."
3. 4.5 to 6.5: carrying four plates, stamps land on the card (8, 16, 24). "Double shifts."
4. 6.5 to 8.5: asleep on the night bus, feet out of her shoes, card reads 64 then 80. "Day 10."
5. 8.5 to 11.0: manager's hand gives an envelope; she tears it open; the pay stub fills the screen, GROSS PAY $1,200, EXAMPLE stamp in the corner. "$1,200."
6. 11.0 to 18.0: four strips snip off one by one and the total ticks down: FEDERAL -$60, SOCIAL SECURITY -$74, MEDICARE -$17, STATE -$39, total $1,010. Her face drops. "Then the taxes came." then "$1,010 left."
7. 18.0 to 21.5: she looks at the stub, then at a framed photo on her phone lock screen (her and mom, no name). "She knew the first thing."
8. 21.5 to 25.5: restaurant booth, the bill arrives, mom reaches for it out of habit. "For 22 years, Mom paid."
9. 25.5 to 30.0: she slides her card on top first; register ding; receipt $46.80 stamped PAID. Payoff. "Tonight, she did."
10. 30.0 to 35.5: mom's eyes well up, she laughs and squeezes her hand; the pay stub on the table gets $46.80 circled in cherry marker. "Best $46.80 she ever spent."
11. 35.5 to 41.5: end card, a perforated pay stub on cream: sprout logo, NUMBERS THAT SAY EVERYTHING / EPISODE 2 · FIRST PAYCHECK, cherry FOLLOW FOR EPISODE 3 pill. Last beat: the time clock chunk, loops to frame 1.

## Episode 4: The last day

Hook (frozen reversal): frame 1 is a close-up of a badge on a glass-door reader that beeps red, ACCESS ENDED · 5:00 PM, with her hands holding a cardboard box. Line "11 years. Badge denied." It reads like a firing; the payoff reveals a retirement.
Story in one sentence: on her last day after 11 years, a woman packs one box and leaves an empty office, her badge stops working at 5 pm, and her whole team is waiting in the lobby to send her off.
Gate: passes (adult, no job loss shown, honest warm ending; the red badge is the hook, the video answers it at 0:31, so the cover is not misleading).

Look: "Glass door at golden hour". Full-bleed office corridor seen through a frosted glass door, warm amber light and glass blue reflections. Device: the badge reader's own small LCD at y 318 to 414 is the counter (SWIPE n, green on dark), with green and red LEDs. The box packing uses a cream label "11 YEARS = 1 BOX". Fonts: Nunito ExtraBold for cards, a drawn seven-segment style for the LCD.
Character: her, early 60s, deep brown skin, short silver natural hair, round tortoiseshell glasses, rust blazer over a cream blouse, gold hoops. Colleagues: six adults of mixed ages and skin tones, simple figures, no names.
Score: vibraphone, warm string pad and soft kick, Eb major, 84 bpm, 15 bars = 42.9 s. Red error beep on frame 1, green chirps on every swipe (rising pitch through the montage), paper and tape sounds on the box, near silence in the empty corridor (footsteps and a distant clock), the red beep again at 5:00, then lights clunk on and the full theme with a cheer swell.

Beats:
