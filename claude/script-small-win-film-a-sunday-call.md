# Script: Small Win Films long film A, "Every Sunday at 6, She Called (Short Film)" (24 Nov 2026)

Status: script shown to Kenny 24 Nov 2026; "let's start the build" (24 Nov) taken as the go. Build in conversation 7 of claude/build-plan-2026-11-24.md.

Story in one sentence: an older woman calls her adult grandson every Sunday at 6, he lets it ring for four weeks while a counter climbs, and in week five he picks up.

Rules: literal story, in order, adults only (grandson is an adult), no kids, no illness detail, no names (screen says GRANDMA only), no Nigerian names, music and sound only plus one spoken line at the end, about 3:00, cold open 5 s or less matching the thumbnail, picture changes every 2 to 3 s in the first 30 s, emotional score (sparse start, swell at the hardest moment, near silence before the turn, resolution at the end), design not used before (check claude/film-log.md Designs used).

Keyword: "short film about grandparents" (24 Nov scan: demand 5, competition Low, long films win 4 times). Title: "Every Sunday at 6, She Called (Short Film)". Check free autocomplete on build day.

Meter: plain count "MISSED SUNDAYS: n" plus a day/time label (SUNDAY 6:00 PM). Big and centred in the first seconds, then shrinks to its corner (safe band).

Scenes:
1. 0:00 to 0:06 hook. A phone buzzes face down on a table, SUNDAY 6:00 PM, screen reads GRANDMA. Counter MISSED SUNDAYS: 3 (big, centred). He turns the phone over and lets it ring out.
2. 0:06 to 0:30. Her kitchen. Landline on the wall, calendar with "Sunday 6" circled, kettle, one cup. She dials, counts the rings, hangs up gently. Counter: 1 (card "3 Sundays earlier." then week 1).
3. 0:30 to 1:10. His week: desk, commute, takeaway dinner, laptop glow. Each time he glances at the phone the day changes. Busy, not cruel.
4. 1:10 to 1:50. Weeks two and three. Her call is the same but small things change: a second cup set out, then put back. Counter: 2, then 3.
5. 1:50 to 2:15. Week four. He sits on the couch at 5:58 with the phone in hand, puts it down. Counter: 4. She leaves the cup out and waits at the table.
6. 2:15 to 2:40. Week five, 6:00 PM. He picks up on the first ring. Her face, then the cup being filled.
7. 2:40 to 3:00. His one line: "Hi, Grandma. Sorry I'm late." Her answer is only the sound of her laughing. End card.

One Short (cut from the film, own hook, own cover, long film set as Related video): "She called every Sunday at 6. He let it ring." (the counter climbing, ending before the pick-up so the cover stays a cliffhanger).

Thumbnail and cover: cliffhanger only, for example the phone face down with GRANDMA ringing and MISSED SUNDAYS: ? (no pick-up, no ending).
