Why Is Bitcoin Crashing? Where Did $1 Trillion Go?

DESCRIPTION
Why is bitcoin crashing, and where did more than $1 trillion actually go? Bitcoin fell from about $126,000 to $62,000. We follow the money.

Bitcoin's total market value dropped from about $2.5 trillion at its October 2025 peak to about $1.23 trillion in mid-November 2026. Most of that money never existed as cash. Market value is just the last price times every coin, so when the price falls, the number falls, even when very little money moves.

But real dollars did move. Early sellers and fund exits took cash off the table: investors pulled about $1.91 billion out of US spot bitcoin ETFs in seven trading days. Over one weekend, more than $2 billion of leveraged crypto bets were liquidated, and that money went to traders on the other side and to the exchanges that closed the bets. Exchanges earned fees on every panic trade, and about $14.2 billion of stablecoin supply left crypto in 30 days.

This is a bitcoin crash explained in plain English: crypto liquidations, ETF outflows, market cap and who gets paid when crypto crashes. It is not financial advice and does not tell you to buy or sell.
