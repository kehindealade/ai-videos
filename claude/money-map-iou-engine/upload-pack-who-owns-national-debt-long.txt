Who Owns the National Debt? It's Not China

DESCRIPTION
Who owns the national debt? America owes about $40.7 trillion, and China holds only about 1.4% of it. Here is who is owed, from Treasury and Federal Reserve data.

The total crossed $40 trillion in August 2026. In this video, every dollar of debt is a certificate, an I.O.U., and we follow it to the four groups that hold it: government trust funds (about $7.8 trillion, 19%), the Federal Reserve (about $4.6 trillion, 11%), foreign holders (about $9.2 trillion, 23%, led by Japan and the U.K.), and everyone else (about $19.1 trillion, 47%): banks, funds, pensions, insurers, state governments and people. You will also see why the China myth stuck, what "held abroad" does and does not mean, how Treasuries get rolled over, and where to check the numbers yourself.

This video is about who holds the debt. It takes no side on whether the debt is good or bad.
