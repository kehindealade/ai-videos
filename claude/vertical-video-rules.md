# Vertical video rules (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), both channels

Read with claude/film-log.md (Small Win Films) or claude/money-map-style-log.md (Money Map) before building any vertical video or cover.

## 1. Full screen, edge to edge (19 Nov 2026)

Meta marked down the Two Cups Facebook Reel for "bordered video" ("There are issues limiting the performance of your post", Content quality minus). Its picture sat inside a window with margins and an outline. The Bad Day Reel, whose picture ran edge to edge, showed "no issues".

- The picture fills all 1080x1920 pixels. Backgrounds (room, map, board) are painted to every edge.
- No frame or outline around the whole picture, no margin of another colour on any side, no picture-in-picture window holding the scene, no letterbox bars.
- Cards, panels, phones and overlays are fine on top of a full-screen picture, as long as they do not wrap the whole frame.
- Check before rendering (contact sheet of stills) and after rendering (sample the four edges of a frame: they must be the scene's background, not a separate border colour).

Fixed so far: Money Map "$16 meal" Reel (the 34 px wooden frame around the chalkboard was removed, re-rendered 19 Nov 2026). Checked and fine: 31 Texts She Never Sent Reel (scenes scaled to fill, room painted to the edges), Bitcoin Reel (map background to the edges).

## 2. Covers and thumbnails are cliffhangers (Kenny, repeated, 19 Nov 2026)

Never show or state the outcome, the cause or the twist. Open a question the video answers early (inside the hook). Test: if someone could tell the whole story from the cover alone, redo it.

## 3. Never repeat a design

New layout, overlays, counter style, hook voice and wording, end card and cover for every video. Log each one in the channel's log.

## 4. Where text goes (Kenny, 22 Nov 2026)

The Thanksgiving Reel's card and cover sat at the top and were hard to read. Every headline, caption, number and price stays inside y 300 to 1340 and x 90 to 990 on 1080x1920; main captions at y 1100 to 1300; covers' question block at y 384 to 900; never essential text in the top 270 px, bottom 400 px or right 70 px. Fonts at least 48 px, at most 32 characters a line, 2 lines, contrast at least 4.5:1, box behind busy areas. Full rules, numbers and sources: claude/readability-and-retention-rules.md. Overlay check before delivery.
